About Us

A law practice built on trust

Golden Creep Law Associates is a law practice based in Chengalpattu, Tamil Nadu, founded by Advocate I. Arun Kumar, B.A., B.L., MBA. We support individuals, families, businesses and banks with practical legal advice and committed courtroom representation.

Our approach is simple: understand the client’s problem fully, explain the law in plain language, and act with diligence and integrity at every stage. With legal training and a management background, we bring both legal accuracy and practical, business-minded thinking to every matter.

Our Services

How we can help you

Legal Opinion for Bank Loans

Sound title opinions so banks and borrowers can proceed with confidence.

  • Title verification & Legal Scrutiny Reports
  • Parent documents & chain of title
  • Encumbrance Certificate verification
  • Patta, Chitta, FMB & revenue records
  • Approved plan & building permission checks
  • Home loans, LAP & business loans
  • Search reports & vetting of deeds

Civil Cases

Careful preparation and strong representation in civil disputes.

  • Property, land & partition suits
  • Money recovery & contract disputes
  • Injunction & declaration suits
  • Divorce, maintenance & custody
  • Wills, probate & legal heir matters
  • Rent control & tenancy disputes
  • Consumer complaints

Criminal Cases

Protecting your rights at every stage of a criminal matter.

  • Complaints & FIR follow-up

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for a bank loan legal opinion?

Usually the sale deed, parent documents, Encumbrance Certificate, patta, tax receipts and approved plan. We’ll share an exact checklist for your property.

How long does a legal opinion take?

Typically a few working days once all documents are received.

Do you handle bail matters urgently?

Yes. Please call our office directly for urgent criminal matters.

Can you help with document registration?

Yes — from drafting the document to completing registration at the Sub-Registrar office.
